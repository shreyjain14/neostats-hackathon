## 1. Scope of Services

Consultant agrees to provide professional services (the "Services") as described in **Exhibit A**. ## 2. Compensation and Payment

**Fees:** Client agrees to pay Consultant a non-refundable retainer of **$15,000** upon execution of this Agreement. Services will be billed at a rate of **$350 per hour**. A minimum of **20** hours will be billed monthly, regardless of the hours worked.

**Payment Terms:** All invoices are due and payable within **seven (7) calendar days** of receipt.

**Late Payment:** Any amounts not paid when due will incur a late fee of **5% per month**, compounded monthly, or the highest rate permitted by law, whichever is lower. Furthermore, Consultant shall have the right to **immediately suspend all Services** without notice until the outstanding balance, including all accrued late fees, is paid in full.

**Non-Refundable:** All fees, retainers, and expenses paid to the Consultant are **non-refundable** under any and all circumstances, including but not limited to the early termination of this Agreement for any reason.

---

## 3. Term and Termination

**Term:** This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year periods unless the Client provides written notice of non-renewal at least **ninety (90) days** prior to the end of the current term.

**Termination by Consultant:** Consultant may terminate this Agreement:
* **For any reason**, upon providing seven (7) days' written notice to the Client.
* **Immediately**, without notice, if the Client breaches any term of this Agreement, fails to make a payment when due, or, in the Consultant's sole judgment, becomes uncooperative.

**Termination by Client:** The Client may not terminate this Agreement for convenience. Termination is only permissible in the event of a material breach by the Consultant that remains uncured for sixty (60) days after the Consultant receives detailed written notice of such breach from the Client.
